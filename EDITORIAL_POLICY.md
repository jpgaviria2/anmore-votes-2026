# Editorial Policy

## Mission

Anmore Votes 2026 helps residents make an informed voting decision by presenting verified election facts and voluntary candidate responses in a consistent, accessible format.

## Equal treatment

- Candidates appear alphabetically within each office.
- Every candidate receives the same invitation, questions, word limits, deadlines, and reminders.
- Council candidate non-response is displayed neutrally as “No questionnaire response published yet.” No school trustee questionnaire is requested because there is only one registered candidate.
- The project does not endorse, score, rank, or recommend candidates.
- The comparison view shows the same selected question for every candidate, in alphabetical order, and labels missing answers as “No response provided.”

## Candidate submissions

- Candidate biographies, portraits, links, and answers are voluntary.
- Candidates send biographies, portraits, links, and answers directly to `election@anmore.me`; the website does not collect candidate information through a public form.
- Identity is matched against the email published in the candidate's official nomination registration. If no email is published, identity is verified through another independently sourced official contact route.
- The candidate receives a final profile proof and must explicitly approve it before publication.
- The candidate must accept the published Candidate Consent and Publication Authorization in writing before publication.
- Candidate answers are not substantively edited. Formatting and minor clarity edits require candidate approval.
- Candidate-supplied material is labelled as such.
- Portraits publish only with confirmation that the candidate may authorize their use.

## Community questions

- Residents send questions and relevant election information directly to `election@anmore.me`; the website does not collect these through a public form.
- Questions are moderated for relevance, specificity, civility, privacy, and potential legal harm.
- Similar questions may be combined without changing their substance.
- Selected questions are sent to all candidates for the relevant office at the same time.
- Anonymous allegations, private personal information, and questions aimed at only one candidate are not published.

## Corrections

- Factual corrections are made promptly and noted when material.
- Candidate responses may be updated at the candidate’s request, with the update date displayed.
- Official election information is checked against the Village of Anmore and CivicInfo BC.

## Independence

The project accepts no candidate advertising or paid placement and is not affiliated with the Village of Anmore, Elections BC, any candidate, or any political organization.

No paid advertisements, sponsored posts, boosted distribution, robocalls, paid canvassing, or preferential placement are authorized. Any proposed paid promotion or material paid production must stop until its status is reassessed with Elections BC and any required third-party sponsor registration, authorization statement, and expense records are in place.

## Privacy

- Collect only information needed for identity verification, moderation, publication approval, corrections, and compliance.
- Do not publish private contact information.
- Retain private information used in a publication decision for at least one year where required by section 35 of B.C.'s Personal Information Protection Act; otherwise delete or de-identify it when no longer needed.
- Direct access, correction, withdrawal, and privacy complaints to `election@anmore.me`.
