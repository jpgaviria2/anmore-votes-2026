We would be pleased to include your biography and any information you would like Anmore residents to know in Anmore Votes 2026, our independent, community-run voter guide.

As the sole registered school trustee candidate, you are simply invited to share the background and public information you feel would be useful to the community.

What you may share

Reply directly to this email with any or all of the following:

  • A biography of up to 1,500 characters
  • Your professional background and community service
  • Experience you would like residents to know about
  • A website or other public links
  • A portrait you have permission to publish
  • Any message or additional information you would like to share with Anmore residents
Reply with your information

Verification and approval
