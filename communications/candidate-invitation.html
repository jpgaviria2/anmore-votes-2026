Hello {{candidate_name}},

You are invited to participate in Anmore Votes 2026, an independent, community-run voter guide where Anmore residents can compare candidate biographies and answers to the same questions.

The platform is neutral by design.

Every candidate for Mayor or Council receives the same invitation, questions, space, and publication process. We do not endorse, rank, score, or recommend candidates.

How to participate

Reply directly to this email with any or all of the following:

  • A biography of up to 1,500 characters
  • Your occupation, professional background, and community service
  • Your campaign website or other public links
  • A portrait you have permission to publish
  • Your numbered answers to the questions below, up to 1,000 characters per answer

Preferred response date: September 25, 2026 at 5:00 p.m. Pacific Time.

This is not a cutoff. Later submissions remain welcome and will be reviewed for publication as the guide is updated.

Reply with your information

Common candidate questions

  1. How long have you been a resident of Anmore?
  2. What are your three measurable priorities for the 2026–2030 term?
  3. What is your position on housing growth and the upcoming Official Community Plan process?
  4. How will you support community recreation in Anmore, including Spirit Park development, year-round recreation activities, and community-led projects such as a pump track?
  5. How should Anmore balance property taxes, service levels, reserves, and long-term infrastructure costs?
  6. How would you address traffic, road safety, transit, visitor pressure, and parking around regional destinations?
  7. What changes would you make to public consultation, meeting transparency, records, and resident communications?
  8. What experience best prepares you to govern, and where would you seek outside expertise?
  9. What potential conflicts of interest would you proactively disclose or recuse yourself from?

Residents may also submit additional community questions to election@anmore.me. Relevant, respectful questions may be moderated and shared equally with every candidate.

Verification and publication

This invitation is being sent to the email published in your official nomination registration. Your submission will not be published until we send you a final profile proof and you approve it. Nothing is published automatically.

Participation is voluntary. Unanswered questions will simply remain blank, and non-participation will be described neutrally.
