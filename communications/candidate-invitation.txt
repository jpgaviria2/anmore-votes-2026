Subject: Invitation to participate in Anmore Votes 2026

Hello {{candidate_name}},

You are invited to participate in Anmore Votes 2026, an independent, community-run voter guide where Anmore residents can compare candidate biographies and answers to the same questions.

The platform is neutral by design. Every candidate for Mayor or Council receives the same invitation, questions, space, and publication process. We do not endorse, rank, score, or recommend candidates.

HOW TO PARTICIPATE

Reply directly to this email with any or all of the following:

- A biography of up to 1,500 characters
- Your occupation, professional background, and community service
- Your campaign website or other public links
- A portrait you have permission to publish
- Your numbered answers to the questions below

COMMON CANDIDATE QUESTIONS

1. How long have you been a resident of Anmore?
2. What are your three measurable priorities for the 2026–2030 term?
3. What is your position on housing growth and the upcoming Official Community Plan process?
4. How will you support community recreation in Anmore, including Spirit Park development, year-round recreation activities, and community-led projects such as a pump track?
5. How should Anmore balance property taxes, service levels, reserves, and long-term infrastructure costs?
6. What should the Village do about drinking-water resilience, stormwater, septic constraints, and future infrastructure capacity?
7. What specific wildfire prevention, evacuation, and emergency-readiness improvements would you support?
8. How would you address traffic, road safety, transit, visitor pressure, and parking around regional destinations?
9. How should Anmore protect forests, waterways, trails, and biodiversity while accommodating legally required housing?
10. What changes would you make to public consultation, meeting transparency, records, and resident communications?
11. How would you work with Metro Vancouver, Port Moody, Belcarra, SD43, the Province, and local First Nations?
12. What experience best prepares you to govern, and where would you seek outside expertise?
13. What potential conflicts of interest would you proactively disclose or recuse yourself from?

VERIFICATION AND PUBLICATION

This invitation is being sent to the email published in your official nomination registration. Your information remains private while it is reviewed. Before publication, we will send you a final profile proof for approval. Nothing is published automatically.

Participation is voluntary. Unanswered questions will simply remain blank, and non-participation will be described neutrally.
