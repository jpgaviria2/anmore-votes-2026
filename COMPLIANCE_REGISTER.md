## Current operating status

- No paid placement, sponsored post, boosted distribution, robocall, paid canvassing, or preferential candidate placement is authorized.
- No candidate, elector organization, or campaign funds or directs the project.
- The project does not claim Elections BC third-party sponsor registration.
- Candidate material is voluntary, identity-verified, consented to in writing, proof-approved, and displayed under equal rules.
- The public website contains no candidate or community submission form and no advertising or behavioural tracking pixels.

## Stop rule

Before introducing paid placement, commercial promotion, material paid production, candidate funding, or coordinated distribution:

1. Stop the proposed activity.
2. Record the proposal and estimated fair-market value below.
3. Reassess it against LECFA and the current Elections BC third-party sponsor guide.
4. Obtain written Elections BC guidance or qualified legal advice when classification is uncertain.
5. If regulated, complete registration before sponsoring the activity and add the required authorization statement and expense/contribution records.
