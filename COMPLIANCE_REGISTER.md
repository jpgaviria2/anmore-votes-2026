# Compliance Register

Last reviewed: September 20, 2026.

This operational register supports the neutral Anmore Votes 2026 project. It is not legal advice or an Elections BC determination.

## Current election period

- Pre-campaign period began July 20, 2026.
- Campaign period runs September 19 through October 17, 2026.
- General voting day is October 17, 2026.

## Current operating status

- No paid placement, sponsored post, boosted distribution, robocall, paid canvassing, candidate funding, or preferential candidate placement is permitted, now or in the future.
- No candidate, elector organization, or campaign funds or directs the project.
- The project does not claim Elections BC third-party sponsor registration.
- Candidate material is voluntary, identity-verified, consented to in writing, proof-approved, and displayed under equal rules.
- The public website contains no candidate or community submission form and no advertising or behavioural tracking pixels.

## Permanent prohibition

Anmore Votes 2026 will never use or accept paid placement, commercial promotion, sponsored distribution, candidate funding, or preferential exposure. Any such proposal must be rejected and recorded as a compliance incident. It cannot be approved through this project.
